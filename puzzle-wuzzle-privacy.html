Privacy Policy for Puzzle Wuzzle

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzle Wuzzle ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Puzzle Wuzzle is developed by FourStarsOutOfFive (contact: gareth.m.nesbitt@gmail.com). By using Puzzle Wuzzle, you agree to the practices described below.

The short version

Puzzle Wuzzle does not ask you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect your name, email, or contact details. The app is free, all 100 levels are free, and it is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, our advertising partner may collect certain device and usage information as described below. A one-time in-app purchase removes all ads and unlocks Free Play.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — which levels you have unlocked, your points total, and your music and sound settings — is saved on your device only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed if you delete the app.

Information collected to show ads

Puzzle Wuzzle uses Google AdMob to display a full-screen ad between levels. Google, acting as our advertising partner, may collect and process the following:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) and similar app- or developer-bounded identifiers, for advertising and analytics.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads have been shown.
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address.
  • Product interaction data, such as app launches and taps.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data.

We do not receive or store this information ourselves in an identifiable form. It is handled by Google. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

App Tracking Transparency (Apple)

On first launch, Puzzle Wuzzle asks for your permission to track using Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, ads are still shown but are non-personalised. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

In-app purchases

Puzzle Wuzzle offers a single one-time purchase that removes all ads and unlocks Free Play, the endless blank-board mode. Every one of the 100 levels is free whether or not you buy it. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. The purchase can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Age rating and children's privacy

Puzzle Wuzzle has an App Store age rating of 4+, meaning it contains no objectionable content and is suitable for players of all ages. Even so, the app is intended for a general audience and is not designed for, or primarily directed to, children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The app requires no account and asks for no personal details. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob; if you decline the tracking request, the ads you see are non-personalised. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take steps to remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold identifiable data about you ourselves, most such requests should be directed to Google using the links above. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings, decline tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or remove ads entirely via the in-app purchase.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

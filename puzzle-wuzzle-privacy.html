Privacy Policy for Puzzle Wuzzle

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzle Wuzzle ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Puzzle Wuzzle is developed by FourStarsOutOfFive (contact: gareth.m.nesbitt@gmail.com). By using Puzzle Wuzzle, you agree to the practices described below.

The short version

Puzzle Wuzzle does not ask you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect your name, email, or contact details. The app is free, all 100 levels are free, and it is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Those ads are non-personalised for every player. We do not ask for permission to track you, and the app does not use your device's advertising identifier. A one-time in-app purchase removes all ads and unlocks Free Play.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — which levels you have unlocked, your points total, and your music and sound settings — is saved on your device only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed if you delete the app.

Non-personalised advertising

Behavioural, or "personalised", advertising is switched off in Puzzle Wuzzle for all players, everywhere, regardless of age or location. We have chosen this deliberately rather than relying on working out which of our players are children. The ads you see are not selected on the basis of a profile built from your interests or your activity in other apps.

Because of this, Puzzle Wuzzle does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and will never show you a prompt asking for permission to track. Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not requested and not used.

Information collected to show ads

Puzzle Wuzzle uses Google AdMob to display a full-screen ad between levels. Even for non-personalised ads, Google, acting as our advertising partner, may collect and process the following:

  • App- or developer-bounded identifiers, used to limit how often you see the same ad and to detect invalid traffic and fraud. These do not identify you across other companies' apps.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads have been shown.
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address, used to serve ads appropriate to your country and to apply the right privacy rules.
  • Product interaction data, such as app launches and taps.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data.

We do not receive or store this information ourselves in an identifiable form. It is handled by Google. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Puzzle Wuzzle shows you a consent message the first time you open it, before any ad is requested. It asks whether you agree to information being stored on or read from your device for advertising purposes, and it offers Do not consent as plainly as it offers Consent.

If you do not consent, no ads are requested and the game plays exactly as it otherwise would. You can reopen these choices at any time using the Privacy options button on the app's main menu. The consent message is provided by Google's User Messaging Platform, and your choice is recorded by Google rather than by us.

In-app purchases

Puzzle Wuzzle offers a single one-time purchase that removes all ads and unlocks Free Play, the endless blank-board mode. Every one of the 100 levels is free whether or not you buy it. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. The purchase can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Age rating and children's privacy

Puzzle Wuzzle has an App Store age rating of 4+, meaning it contains no objectionable content and is suitable for players of all ages. The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed for, or primarily directed to, children.

We recognise that a free, colourful puzzle game may well be played by children. Rather than attempt to judge who is and is not a child, we apply the stricter standard to everyone: behavioural advertising is off for all players, no tracking permission is sought, and the advertising identifier is not used. The app requires no account and asks for no personal details.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take steps to remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold identifiable data about you ourselves, most such requests should be directed to Google using the links above. Within the app you can reopen your consent choices at any time via Privacy options on the main menu, or remove advertising entirely with the in-app purchase.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

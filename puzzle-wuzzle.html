Puzzle Wuzzle

A calm ball-fitting puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Twelve coloured pieces, fifty-five sockets, and one question: can you make them all fit? Early levels start with most of the board already filled, so you learn the shapes as you go. By level 100 you are staring at a blank board with all twelve pieces in your hand.

Runway Rush

A fast, friendly air-traffic-control game for iPhone and iPad.

Guide planes safely to their runways by drawing flight paths with your finger. Clear the skies, chase your best score, and keep the runways rushing.

Remove Ads

Runway Rush is free to play with occasional ads between waves. A one-time in-app purchase removes all ads and unlocks the full, uninterrupted game. Players who previously bought the paid version keep the ad-free experience automatically.
